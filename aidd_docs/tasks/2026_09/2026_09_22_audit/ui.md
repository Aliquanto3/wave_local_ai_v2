# Codebase Audit: ui

Caveat labels are wired in most places, but the runtime screen attaches the `unreliable` flag to a metric it does not gate. The comparison and energy screens also drop caveats and provenance that the per-run screens carry.

- **Date**: 2026_09_22
- **Scope**: `frontend/src/` (App, KeyGate, Absent, labels/, views RunsList, quality, runtime, energy, comparison) plus the `read_model.py` field lists that decide what reaches each view
- **Health**: fair
- **Findings**: 1 critical, 8 warning, 6 minor

Health: `good` = no critical findings; `fair` = critical findings exist but are isolated and addressable; `poor` = systemic or widespread critical findings.
